Prana Health

Preventive Healthcare Platform

High-fidelity UI mockups for continuous health monitoring and early risk detection

Screen 1
Health Risk Trajectory
Primary screen visualizing 6-12 month risk projections for key conditions. Shows direction of health trends with a subtle baseline comparison.
  • Risk visualization by condition
  • Trajectory projection chart
  • Improving/worsening indicators
  • Baseline comparison
Screen 2
Daily Action Confidence
Single daily score (0-100) summarizing how today's behavior impacts long-term health. Includes top contributors and one corrective action.
  • Confidence score visualization
  • Top 2 contributing behaviors
  • One corrective action
  • Daily summary metrics
Screen 3
Silent Risk Detection
Background AI flags early risk patterns before symptoms appear. Uses plain language, no medical jargon.
  • Early pattern detection
  • Plain language explanations
  • Actionable suggestions
  • Pattern details & trends
Screen 4
Doctor-Ready Health Brief
One-click, auto-generated clinical summary with trends, anomalies, and suggested questions for consultations.
  • 30-day health summary
  • Key trends & anomalies
  • Suggested questions
  • PDF export & sharing

Design Principles

🎯
One Insight Per Screen
Each screen focuses on a single, clear insight with one consequence and one action
⚡
10-Second Understanding
Default view must be comprehensible in under 10 seconds
🧘
Calm & Clinical
Trust-first design with no gamification noise or overwhelming dashboards
📱
Mobile-First
Designed for mobile devices with progressive disclosure of information
